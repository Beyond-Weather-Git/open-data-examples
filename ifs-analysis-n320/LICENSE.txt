Modifications: This dataset was modified from the original ECMWF IFS operational analysis by Beyond Weather.
- A selection of variables and pressure levels was retrieved from the ECMWF MARS archive, interpolated by MARS to the N320 reduced Gaussian grid.
- The GRIB data was converted to Zarr v3 in an Icechunk repository, and variables were renamed to long descriptive names.
- Values were bit-rounded (lossy) to the number of mantissa bits in each variable's "bwdl_keepbits" attribute before compression.
